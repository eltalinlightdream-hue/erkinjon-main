Listening Script – Section 2

Part 2. You will hear a talk about volunteering opportunities at local festivals. First you have some time to look at questions eleven to sixteen.

Now listen carefully and answer questions eleven to sixteen.

Hello everyone, and welcome to this information session about volunteering at our city's festivals. We have a range of opportunities available across several different activities.

For those of you who enjoy being out and about, we have volunteers who walk around the town centre during festival season. Their main role is giving advice to visitors – helping people find venues, answering questions about events, that sort of thing.

At our concert venues, volunteers help in a very specific way – they help people find their seats, which is especially important when large crowds arrive at once.

We're also looking for volunteers to get involved with our community groups initiative. This is about encouraging cooperation between local organisations – getting different groups to work together on joint festival projects.

Helping with our magazine is another option. Volunteers in this role collect feedback on events from audience members and festival-goers, which feeds into our monthly publication.

For something a bit different, we have volunteers who participate at our lunches for retired people in the community. Their role is essentially providing entertainment – organising activities and making the events enjoyable.

And finally, for those with digital skills, there's always help needed with the website. This involves providing publicity about a council service – updating content and spreading the word about what the council offers.

Before you hear the rest of the talk, you have some time to look at questions seventeen to twenty.

Now listen and answer questions seventeen to twenty.

Now let me tell you about our three main festivals. The music festival is the most established and the book festival is our newest. However, it's actually the science festival that requires the largest number of volunteers, due to the interactive exhibits that need constant supervision.

As for what we look for in volunteers – the most important thing is interpersonal skills. You'll be dealing with the public constantly, so being friendly and communicative is essential.

In terms of start dates, new volunteers this year will begin in the week starting the 9th of September.

And finally, I'm pleased to announce that our next annual volunteer appreciation event will be a boat trip along the river – always a highlight of the year!

Thank you everyone. That is the end of Part 2.
