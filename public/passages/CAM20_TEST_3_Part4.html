Listening Script – Section 4

Inclusive design means designing products and environments that can be used by as wide a range of people as possible, without requiring any adaptation.

Different from universal design, which includes people with cognitive problems.

Desks with adjustable height help avoid back and neck problems. Lever-style taps in public toilets are easier to use. For elderly users, designers avoid using blue in digital interfaces. Products allow voice commands.

Seatbelts are problematic for pregnant women. PPE jackets don't fit women's shoulders. Worst is PPE for female police officers. Office temperature is too cold for women.
