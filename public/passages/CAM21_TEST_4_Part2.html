Listening Script – Section 2

Part 2. You will hear a talk about a football stadium tour and the history of football. First you have some time to look at questions eleven to fourteen.

Now listen carefully and answer questions eleven to fourteen.

Welcome to Linfield United Football Club! I'm thrilled you could join us for today's stadium tour.

For those of you visiting with children – great news. Today we have a special competition running for children, where they can win signed merchandise. And for safety reasons, parents must stay with children at all times during the tour.

Now, this year we've made some exciting additions. We've launched a brand new VIP tour for the first time – this takes you behind the scenes to areas never opened before. We've also introduced a new audio guide system, so you can explore at your own pace with commentary in 12 languages.

Before you hear the rest of the talk, you have some time to look at questions fifteen to twenty.

Now listen and answer questions fifteen to twenty.

Let me take you through some key moments in football history. In 1870 – a pivotal year – goalkeepers were officially introduced. Before this, any player could handle the ball.

In 1874, there were actually two separate changes made to the rules of the game in the same year, making it quite a landmark year for football governance.

Then in 1875, the cross-bar was introduced – a significant change to the design of the goal. Before this, a tape was used instead.

Moving to 1877 – this was the year when clubs finally agreed on a standard length for a game: 90 minutes. Before this, match lengths varied enormously.

In 1878, an extraordinary innovation – the first use of electric lights for a match. This allowed football to be played after dark for the first time.

And in 1880, clubs began charging spectators an admission fee. The introduction of a fee for spectators transformed football from a pastime into a commercial enterprise.

That is the end of Part 2.
