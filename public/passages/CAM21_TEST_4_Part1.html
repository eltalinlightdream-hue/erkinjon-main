Listening Script – Section 1

Part 1. You will hear a conversation between two friends, Helen and Dan, where Helen gives Dan advice about visiting a city. First you have some time to look at questions one to six.

Now listen carefully and answer questions one to six.

Dan: Hi Helen! You know that area well – we're planning a family visit next month.
Helen: Oh lovely! I can definitely give you some tips. First, accommodation – have you booked anywhere?
Dan: Not yet. Any suggestions?
Helen: The Kings Hotel on George Street is perfect for families – really central.
Dan: Kings Hotel, George Street. What's the cost?
Helen: For a family room, it's approximately £125 per night. Very reasonable.
Dan: Perfect. What about things to do?
Helen: I'd definitely recommend a walking tour of the city centre. They start from Carlton Square – brilliant way to see everything.
Dan: Walking tour, Carlton Square. And what about getting out to see the fort?
Helen: Yes, the old fort is beautiful. You should go by boat – there's a lovely river trip.
Dan: Boat trip to the fort. The kids will love that!

Before you hear the rest of the conversation, you have some time to look at questions seven to ten.

Now listen and answer questions seven to ten.

Dan: What about museums?
Helen: The Science Museum is excellent. I'd suggest going on Tuesday – it's much less crowded.
Dan: Tuesday. Noted.
Helen: And keep an eye out for the new exhibition about space that's opening soon. Perfect for children.
Dan: Space exhibition – brilliant. What about food?
Helen: Clacton Market is amazing – particularly good for vegetarian food. Lots of plant-based options.
Dan: Good for vegetarian. My wife will be pleased. Any time restrictions?
Helen: Yes, the food stalls close early – make sure you have lunch before 2.30 p.m. or you'll miss out.
Dan: Before 2.30. Got it.
Helen: And for theatre tickets, check bargaintickets.com – you can save up to 75% on prices there.
Dan: 75%. That's impressive. Any free activities?
Helen: Blakewell Gardens is gorgeous – there's the Roots Music Festival on while you'll be there. And if you climb Telegraph Hill, you get a fantastic view of the port.
Dan: Port view from Telegraph Hill. Wonderful. Thank you so much, Helen!

That is the end of Part 1. You now have one minute to check your answers to Part 1.
