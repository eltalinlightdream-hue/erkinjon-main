Listening Script – Section 1

Part 1. You will hear a conversation between a woman called Sarah and a man at a furniture rental company. First you have some time to look at questions one to six.

Now listen carefully and answer questions one to six.

Agent: Good morning, Furniture Finder helpline, how can I help?
Sarah: Hi, I'm looking to rent some furniture for my new flat. Can you tell me about the options available?
Agent: Of course! I'll go through a few companies with you. First, there's Peak Rentals.
Sarah: Peak Rentals. What are their prices like?
Agent: Their prices range from $105 up to $239 per month, depending on what you need.
Sarah: And what's included?
Agent: The furniture is very modern – all contemporary designs. They deliver within one to two days. And right now they have a special offer – you get a free lamp with every living room set you rent.
Sarah: That's nice. Do they offer any other services?
Agent: Yes, they also offer a cleaning service for their rented furniture.
Sarah: Good. What's the next company?
Agent: There's Aaron and Oliver. They're in the mid-range price-wise. They charge a 12% monthly fee for any damage to the furniture.
Sarah: OK. What about the cheapest option?
Agent: That would be Larch Furniture. They offer the cheapest prices for renting, especially for electronic items like TVs and kitchen appliances.
Sarah: Electronic items. What are their terms?
Agent: You need to have your own insurance. And the minimum contract length is six months.

Before you hear the rest of the conversation, you have some time to look at questions seven to ten.

Now listen and answer questions seven to ten.

Sarah: Are there any other companies?
Agent: Yes, there's Space Rentals. Their prices change quite frequently, so they recommend you check the app for the most up-to-date prices.
Sarah: The app. OK. And what's their policy if I'm not happy with something?
Agent: They allow exchanges within seven days of delivery. So if the furniture doesn't suit your space, you can swap it.
Sarah: That's a good policy. Thank you so much for your help.
Agent: My pleasure!

That is the end of Part 1. You now have one minute to check your answers to Part 1.
