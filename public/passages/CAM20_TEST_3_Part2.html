Listening Script – Section 2

Part 2. You will hear a talk about an archaeological dig at Bidcaster.

This project was actually started by a national charity dedicated to preserving local heritage. The local council got involved later.

The dig began after heavy rain washed away some soil near the castle walls, revealing an old gold coin.

We found some old records in a local archive – documents that mentioned a settlement in this exact location.

We're still hoping to find pieces of jewellery, which were commonly produced in medieval settlements.

On the other side of the river from the castle, we found the clear outline of fields – evidence of a farming community.

After this summer, we plan to start organising school visits.

Near the river at position B – bridge foundations. At position A (left side) – a rubbish pit. At position G (centre, near dig) – the meeting hall. At position E (east, near trees) – a fish pond.
