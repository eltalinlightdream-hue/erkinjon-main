### What was imported

- Kinds: 386 essays, 18 PDF/DOCX file posts, 5 image-only posts (essay screenshots), 2 posts with a YouTube explanation.
- Questions: 113 have the original task wording (from the post, its photo caption or the attached file); **298 were reconstructed** from the answer's introduction because the post had no question. Those show a small note on the detail page saying the wording was reconstructed.
- Types: Task 1 → Bar Chart 85, Line Graph 53, Table 48, Mixed 30, Pie Chart 25, Map 19, Process 6; Task 2 → Agree/Disagree 52, Discussion 35, Advantages/Disadvantages 16, Positive/Negative Development 14, Problem/Solution 11, Two-Part Question 9, Direct Question 7, Other 1.
- Band score: found on 16 posts (your own estimates, e.g. "Band 8.0 or 8.5" → `8.0–8.5`).
- Dates: shown only for the 41 posts written directly in the channel. The rest were bulk-forwarded on 15 Nov 2026, so their real date isn't in the export and is hidden.
- Hashtags, links and emoji were stripped from displayed text; trailing lines such as "354 words" / "Band 8.0" were moved out of the essay body. Essay wording itself was not edited.
- Images converted to WebP (480px thumbnails + ≤1400px full size) and lazy-loaded; existing site images got WebP thumbnails too.
